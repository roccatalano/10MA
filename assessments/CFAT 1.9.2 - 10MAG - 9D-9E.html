Find the five-number summary for this ordered data set.
$7, 9, 11, 12, 13, 13, 18, 19, 35, 42, 45, 46, 61, 62, 78$
$15$ values: median is the $8$th value $= 19$
$Q_1 = 12$ (middle of the lower $7$), $Q_3 = 46$ (middle of the upper $7$)
Min $= 7$, $Q_1 = 12$, median $= 19$, $Q_3 = 46$, max $= 78$

Question 5 9D.2 · L2   2 marks
Consider this data set: $2, 6, 3, 8, 9, 10, 14, 19, 4, 8, 22, 11, 13, 17, 4, 19, 7, 7, 10$.
Find the lower quartile, median and upper quartile, calculate the values of the lower and upper fences, and determine whether there are any outliers in this data set.
$2, 3, 4, 4, 6, 7, 7, 8, 8, 9, 10, 10, 11, 13, 14, 17, 19, 19, 22$
Median $= 9$, $Q_1 = 6$, $Q_3 = 14$, IQR $= 8$
Lower fence $= 6 - 1.5 \times 8 = -6$, upper fence $= 14 + 1.5 \times 8 = 26$
No outliers.
Oxford Maths 10 VC (2022), Exercise 10B Q2b–d (p. 509); adapted: parts b–d combined

$3, 5, 10, 12, 20, 22, 25, 29, 34, 34, 37, 42, 51, 55$
