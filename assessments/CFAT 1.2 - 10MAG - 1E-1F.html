Question 9 1F.3 · L1   1 mark

Give the equation of the straight line that has gradient $\dfrac{4}{3}$ and passes through the point $(6, 3)$.

$3 = \dfrac{4}{3}(6) + c$
$3 = 8 + c$, $c = -5$
$y = \dfrac{4}{3}x - 5$
