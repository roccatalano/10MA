Question 3 1G.1 · L2   1 mark

For the line segment joining $(2, 4)$ and $(6, 8)$, find the length (to two decimal places where applicable).

$d = \sqrt{(6 - 2)^2 + (8 - 4)^2}$
$= \sqrt{32} \approx 5.66$
Cambridge Essential Mathematics 9 (3rd Ed), Ch 4 review, short answer Q10a ii (p. 322); adapted: stem reworded for a single part
Question 4 1G.2 · L3   2 marks

Find the missing coordinate, $n$, if the midpoint of the line segment joining $(n, 1)$ and $(1, -3)$ is $(3.5, -1)$.

$\dfrac{n + 1}{2} = 3.5$
$n + 1 = 7$
$n = 6$
Cambridge Essential Mathematics 9 (3rd Ed), Ch 4 review, short answer Q11c (p. 322); adapted: stem reworded for a single part
Question 5 1G.3 · L2   2 marks

The following three points are plotted on a Cartesian plane: $A(-2, 3)$, $B(1, 12)$ and $C(7, 10)$. Show that line segments drawn between these points form a right-angled triangle with the points $A$, $B$ and $C$ as vertices.

$AB^2 = 3^2 + 9^2 = 90$, $BC^2 = 6^2 + 2^2 = 40$
$AC^2 = 9^2 + 7^2 = 130$
$90 + 40 = 130$, so right-angled at $B$
Oxford Maths 10 VC (2022), Exercise 4E Q20a (p. 196)
Question 6 1H.2 · L3   2 marks

Write an equation for a line that is parallel to the graph of $y = 3x + 2$ and passes through $(-3, -2)$.

$m = 3$
$-2 = 3(-3) + c$, $c = 7$
$y = 3x + 7$
Oxford Maths 10 VC (2022), Ch 4 review, short answer Q13b (p. 208); adapted: stem reworded for a single part
Question 7 1H.3 · L2   1 mark

Write an equation for a line that is perpendicular to the graph of $y = 2x - 7$; with $y$-intercept of $-4$.

$m = -\dfrac{1}{2}$, $c = -4$
$y = -\dfrac{1}{2}x - 4$
