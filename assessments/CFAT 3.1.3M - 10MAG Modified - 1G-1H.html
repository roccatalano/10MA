Question 1 1G.1 · L0   1 mark
Find the length of the segment joining $(1, 2)$ and $(3, 4)$ correct to two decimal places.
$d = \sqrt{2^2 + 2^2} = \sqrt{8}$
$\approx 2.83$

Question 2 1G.2 · L0   1 mark
Find the midpoint $M(x, y)$ of the line segment joining $(3, 0)$ and $(5, 2)$.
$M = \left(\dfrac{3 + 5}{2}, \dfrac{0 + 2}{2}\right) = (4, 1)$
Cambridge Essential Mathematics 9 (3rd Ed), Exercise 4H Q1c (p. 293); adapted: stem reworded for a single part
Question 3 1G.3 · L0   1 mark
$M$ is the midpoint of points $A$ and $B$. $A$ is $(4, 2)$ and $M$ is $(6, 1)$. Find the coordinates of $B$.
$\dfrac{4 + x}{2} = 6$, $x = 8$; $\dfrac{2 + y}{2} = 1$, $y = 0$
$B(8, 0)$
Cambridge Essential Mathematics 9 (3rd Ed), Exercise 4H Q3 row 1 (p. 293); adapted: table row written as a sentence (Year 9 finds coordinates from a midpoint, not a distance)

$m_2 = -\dfrac{3}{2}$

Question 5 1H.2 · L0   1 mark

Question 6 1H.3 · L0   1 mark
Find the equation of the line that is perpendicular to $y = 3x - 2$ and passes through $(0, 3)$.
$m = -\dfrac{1}{3}$, $c = 3$
$y = -\dfrac{1}{3}x + 3$
Cambridge Essential Mathematics 9 (3rd Ed), Exercise 4I Q2a (p. 297); adapted: stem reworded for a single part
