Question 1 1K.1 · L0   2 marks
Mila plants $820$ hectares of potatoes and corn. To maximise his profit he plants $140$ hectares more of potatoes than of corn. How many hectares of each does he plant?
$p + c = 820$, $p = c + 140$
$2c + 140 = 820$, $c = 340$
$p = 480$: $480$ ha potatoes, $340$ ha corn
Cambridge Essential Mathematics 9 (3rd Ed), Exercise 2K Q2 (p. 153)
Question 2 1K.1 · L0   2 marks
Mal bought three bottles of milk and four bags of chips for a total of $17. At the same shop, Barbara bought one bottle of milk and five bags of chips for $13. Find how much each bottle of milk and each bag of chips cost.
$3m + 4c = 17$, $m + 5c = 13$
$m = 13 - 5c$: $39 - 15c + 4c = 17$, $c = 2$
$m = 3$: milk $3, chips $2
Cambridge Essential Mathematics 9 (3rd Ed), Exercise 2K Q3 (p. 154); adapted: steps a–d left out
