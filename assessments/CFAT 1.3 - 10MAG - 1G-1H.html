Question 4 1G.3 · L1   2 marks

Find the missing coordinate, $n$, if the line segment joining $(-2, 2)$ and $(4, n)$ has length $10$, $n > 0$.

$10^2 = 6^2 + (n - 2)^2$
$(n - 2)^2 = 64$, $n - 2 = \pm 8$
$n = 10$ (as $n > 0$)
Cambridge Essential Mathematics 9 (3rd Ed), Ch 4 review, short answer Q11b (p. 322); adapted: stem reworded for a single part
