Short answer · 2 mk

$y = 4$ broken; $y = -x$ solid; meet at $(-4, 4)$
Jacaranda Maths Quest 10 + 10A (2022), Exercise 4.9 Q3a (p. 275); adapted: stem reworded for a single part
Short answer · 2 mk

Sketch the following pair of inequalities: $3x + 4y < 24$ and $y > 2x - 5$.

$3x + 4y = 24$: $(0, 6)$, $(8, 0)$, broken
$y = 2x - 5$: $(0, -5)$, $(2.5, 0)$, broken
intersection: $3x + 8x - 20 = 24$, $(4, 3)$
Jacaranda Maths Quest 10 + 10A (2022), Exercise 4.9 Q5a (p. 275); adapted: stem reworded for a single part

vertices: $(-2, -3)$, $(2.6, -3)$, $(-2, 8.5)$
