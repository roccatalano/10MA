1D.1
I can combine algebraic fractions under addition or subtraction.
Short answer · 1 mk

Simplify $\dfrac{x - 2}{4} - \dfrac{3 - x}{5}$.

$= \dfrac{5(x - 2) - 4(3 - x)}{20}$
$= \dfrac{5x - 10 - 12 + 4x}{20} = \dfrac{9x - 22}{20}$
Oxford Maths 10 VC (2022), Ch 2 review, short answer Q10b (p. 99); adapted: stem reworded for a single part
Short answer · 1 mk

Simplify $\dfrac{4x - 1}{12} - \dfrac{3 - 2x}{8}$.

$= \dfrac{2(4x - 1) - 3(3 - 2x)}{24}$
$= \dfrac{8x - 2 - 9 + 6x}{24} = \dfrac{14x - 11}{24}$
Oxford Maths 10 VC (2022), Ch 2 review, short answer Q10c (p. 99); adapted: stem reworded for a single part
Multiple choice · 2 mk

The expression $\dfrac{2}{x + 1} - \dfrac{1}{(x + 1)^2}$ can be simplified to:

  1. A $\dfrac{1}{x + 1}$
  2. B $\dfrac{1}{(x + 1)^2}$
  3. C $\dfrac{x - 1}{(x + 1)^2}$
  4. D $\dfrac{2x}{(x + 1)^2}$
  5. E $\dfrac{2x + 1}{(x + 1)^2}$
$= \dfrac{2(x + 1) - 1}{(x + 1)^2} = \dfrac{2x + 1}{(x + 1)^2}$ (E)
Jacaranda Maths Quest 10 + 10A (2022), Topic 2 pre-test Q5 (as used in CFAT 1.1)
1D.2
I can solve linear equations involving algebraic fractions.
Short answer · 1 mk

Solve $\dfrac{4 + x}{3} = \dfrac{2x - 5}{3}$.

$4 + x = 2x - 5$
$x = 9$
Oxford Maths 10 VC (2022), Ch 4 review, short answer Q2a (p. 208); adapted: stem reworded for a single part
Short answer · 1 mk

Solve $\dfrac{5 - 3x}{2} = \dfrac{2x + 5}{3}$. Leave your answer as a fraction if appropriate.

$3(5 - 3x) = 2(2x + 5)$
$15 - 9x = 4x + 10$
$5 = 13x,\ x = \dfrac{5}{13}$
Oxford Maths 10 VC (2022), Ch 4 review, short answer Q2b (p. 208); adapted: stem reworded for a single part
Short answer · 2 mk

Solve $\dfrac{4x}{9} + \dfrac{7}{3} = \dfrac{7}{4} - \dfrac{11x}{12}$.

$\times 36$: $16x + 84 = 63 - 33x$
$49x = -21$
$x = -\dfrac{3}{7}$
Oxford Maths 10 VC (2022), Exercise 4A Q9d (p. 162); adapted: stem reworded for a single part
