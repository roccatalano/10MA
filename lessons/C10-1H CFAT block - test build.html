The gradient of a line perpendicular to $y = 4x - 7$ would be:

  1. A $\dfrac{1}{4}$
  2. B $4$
  3. C $-4$
  4. D $-\dfrac{1}{4}$
  5. E $-7$
$m_2 = -\dfrac{1}{4}$ (D)
Cambridge Essential Mathematics 9 (3rd Ed), Ch 4 review, multiple-choice Q9 (p. 324)
Multiple choice · 1 mk

A line parallel to the line $3x + 2y = 6$ would have a gradient of:

  1. A $\dfrac{2}{3}$
  2. B $-3$
  3. C $-\dfrac{3}{2}$
  4. D $\dfrac{3}{2}$
  5. E $-\dfrac{2}{3}$
$y = -\dfrac{3}{2}x + 3$; parallel: same gradient (C)
Oxford Maths 10 VC (2022), Ch 4 review, multiple-choice Q10 (p. 207)
Short answer · 2 mk

Four points are plotted on a Cartesian plane: $A(-2, 1)$, $B(5, 4)$, $C(-6, -4)$ and $D(8, 2)$. Is line segment $AB$ parallel to line segment $CD$?

$m_{AB} = \dfrac{4 - 1}{5 - (-2)} = \dfrac{3}{7}$
$m_{CD} = \dfrac{2 - (-4)}{8 - (-6)} = \dfrac{6}{14} = \dfrac{3}{7}$
Yes, equal gradients so $AB \parallel CD$
Oxford Maths 10 VC (2022), Exercise 4E Q19a (p. 196); adapted: stem reworded for a single part
1H.2
I can find the equation of a parallel line.
Short answer · 1 mk

Determine the linear equation that is parallel to the line $y = 2x - 1$ and passes through the point $(0, 4)$.

$m = 2$, $c = 4$
$y = 2x + 4$
Cambridge Essential Mathematics 9 (3rd Ed), Ch 4 review, short answer Q12a (p. 322); adapted: stem reworded for a single part
Short answer · 1 mk

Write an equation for a line that is parallel to the graph of $y = -4x + 5$; with $y$-intercept of $-3$.

$m = -4$, $c = -3$
$y = -4x - 3$
Oxford Maths 10 VC (2022), Ch 4 review, short answer Q12a (p. 208); adapted: stem reworded for a single part
Short answer · 2 mk

Write an equation for a line that is parallel to the graph of $y = 3x + 2$ and passes through $(-3, -2)$.

$m = 3$
$-2 = 3(-3) + c$, $c = 7$
$y = 3x + 7$
Oxford Maths 10 VC (2022), Ch 4 review, short answer Q13b (p. 208); adapted: stem reworded for a single part
