1E.1
I can determine if a point is on a straight line.
Multiple choice · 1 mk

The point that is not on the straight line $y = -2x + 3$ is:

  1. A $(1, 1)$
  2. B $(0, 3)$
  3. C $(2, 0)$
  4. D $(-2, 7)$
  5. E $(3, -3)$
$x = 2$: $y = -4 + 3 = -1 \neq 0$
(C)
Cambridge Essential Mathematics 9 (3rd Ed), Ch 4 review, multiple-choice Q4 (p. 324)
Short answer · 1 mk

Determine if the point $(2, 5)$ is the intersection point of the graphs of $y = 4x - 3$ and $y = -2x + 6$.

$4(2) - 3 = 5$ ✓
$-2(2) + 6 = 2 \neq 5$
No, $(2, 5)$ is not on both lines.
Cambridge Essential Mathematics 9 (3rd Ed), Ch 4 review, short answer Q13a (p. 322); adapted: stem reworded for a single part
Short answer · 2 mk

Determine if the point $(2, 5)$ is the intersection point of the graphs of $3x - 2y = -4$ and $2x + y = 9$.

$3(2) - 2(5) = -4$ ✓
$2(2) + 5 = 9$ ✓
Yes, $(2, 5)$ is the intersection point.
Cambridge Essential Mathematics 9 (3rd Ed), Ch 4 review, short answer Q13b (p. 322); adapted: stem reworded for a single part
1E.2
I can find the gradient and $y$-intercept from a straight line equation.
Multiple choice · 1 mk

The linear graph that does not have a gradient of $3$ is:

  1. A $y = 3x + 7$
  2. B $\dfrac{y}{3} = x + 2$
  3. C $2y - 6x = 1$
  4. D $y - 3x = 4$
  5. E $3x + y = -1$
$3x + y = -1 \Rightarrow y = -3x - 1$, $m = -3$
(E)
Cambridge Essential Mathematics 9 (3rd Ed), Ch 4 review, multiple-choice Q5 (p. 324)
Multiple choice · 1 mk

The gradient of the line $12x + 4y = 8$ is:

  1. A $3$
  2. B $-3$
  3. C $12$
  4. D $-12$
  5. E $4$
$4y = -12x + 8$, $y = -3x + 2$
$m = -3$ (B)
Oxford Maths 10 VC (2022), Ch 4 review, multiple-choice Q6 (p. 207)
Short answer · 2 mk

State the value of the gradient and the $y$-intercept of $2y - 3x - 8 = 0$.

$2y = 3x + 8$
$y = \dfrac{3}{2}x + 4$
$m = \dfrac{3}{2}$, $y$-intercept $(0, 4)$
Cambridge Essential Mathematics 9 (3rd Ed), Ch 4 review, short answer Q7d (p. 321); adapted: stem reworded for a single part
