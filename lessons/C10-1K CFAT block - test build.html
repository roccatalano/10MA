1K.1
I can set up and solve simultaneous equations.
Short answer · 2 mk

The sum of the ages of Billy and his sister is 32. If Billy is 8 years older than his sister, what are their ages?

$b + s = 32$, $b - s = 8$
$2b = 40$, $b = 20$, $s = 12$
Billy is 20, his sister is 12.
Cambridge Essential Mathematics 9 (3rd Ed), Ch 2 review, short answer Q15 (p. 165)
Short answer · 2 mk

The perimeter around the top of a rectangular billiard table is 810 cm. The length is twice the width. Use $l$ and $w$ to write two linear equations, then solve them to find the dimensions of the top of the billiard table.

$2l + 2w = 810$, $l = 2w$
$6w = 810$, $w = 135$
$l = 270$ cm, $w = 135$ cm
Oxford Maths 10 VC (2022), Exercise 4F Q14a–c (p. 204); adapted: three parts combined
Short answer · 2 mk

Sophie has decided to make hand-painted cards. She invests $60 in a set of paintbrushes and enough paint to make 40 cards. The cost of each plain white card is $2 and she plans to sell the painted cards for $6 each. The cost is $a = 2n + 60$ and the money received is $a = 6n$. What are the coordinates of the point of intersection of the two graphs? What does this tell you?

$6n = 2n + 60$, $4n = 60$, $n = 15$
$a = 90$: $(15, 90)$
Selling 15 cards recovers the $90 spent (break even).
Oxford Maths 10 VC (2022), Exercise 4F Q13d (p. 204); adapted: parts a, b given in the stem
