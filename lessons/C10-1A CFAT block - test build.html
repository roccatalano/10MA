1A.1
I can simplify expressions using the four operations $+, -, \times, \div$.
Multiple choice · 1 mk

The simplified form of $6ab + 14a \div 2 - 2ab$ is:

  1. A $8ab$
  2. B $8ab + 7a$
  3. C $ab + 7a$
  4. D $4ab + 7a$
  5. E $4ab + \dfrac{7a}{2}$
$14a \div 2 = 7a,\ 6ab - 2ab = 4ab$
$= 4ab + 7a$ (D)
Cambridge Essential Mathematics 9 (3rd Ed), Ch 2 review, multiple-choice Q2 (p. 165)
Multiple choice · 1 mk

Which of the following expressions is equivalent to $a^2b - 2ab - ab^2 + 5ab$?

  1. A $7ab$
  2. B $a^2b + 3ab + ab^2$
  3. C $3ab$
  4. D $3a^2b^2$
  5. E $a^2b + 3ab - ab^2$
$-2ab + 5ab = 3ab$; $a^2b$ and $-ab^2$ are not like terms
$= a^2b + 3ab - ab^2$ (E)
Oxford Maths 10 VC (2022), Ch 2 review, multiple-choice Q3 (p. 98)
Short answer · 2 mk

Simplify $ab^2 + (ab)^2 - 3a^2b^2 + 2a^2b - 4b^2a$.

$= ab^2 + a^2b^2 - 3a^2b^2 + 2a^2b - 4ab^2$
$= -3ab^2 - 2a^2b^2 + 2a^2b$
Oxford Maths 10 VC (2022), Ch 2 review, short answer Q4b (p. 99)

I can expand brackets using the distributive law.

Cambridge Essential Mathematics 9 (3rd Ed), Ch 2 review, short answer Q4d (p. 164)
Short answer · 1 mk

Expand and simplify $-2x(3 - x) + 5x(2 - 3x)$.

$= -6x + 2x^2 + 10x - 15x^2$
$= -13x^2 + 4x$
Oxford Maths 10 VC (2022), Ch 2 review, short answer Q9b (p. 99)

Expand and simplify $x(x + 1) - x(x - 1) + x(x - 3)$.

$= x^2 + x - x^2 + x + x^2 - 3x$
$= x^2 - x$
Oxford Maths 10 VC (2022), Ch 2 review, short answer Q9c (p. 99)
1A.3
I can factorise simple algebraic expressions with a common factor.
Short answer · 1 mk

Factorise $24 + 16g$.

$= 8(3 + 2g)$
Oxford Maths 10 VC (2022), Exercise 2F Q1g (p. 78)
Multiple choice · 1 mk

What is the fully factorised form of $8x^2y^3 + 12x^3y$?

  1. A $8x^2y(y^2 + 4x)$
  2. B $4x^2y(2y^2 + 3x)$
  3. C $4xy(2xy^2 + 3x^2)$
  4. D $4xy(2y^2 + 3x)$
  5. E $8x^3y^3(x + y^2)$
HCF $= 4x^2y$
$= 4x^2y(2y^2 + 3x)$ (B)
Oxford Maths 10 VC (2022), Ch 2 review, multiple-choice Q7 (p. 98)
Short answer · 2 mk

Factorise $-4b^3 + 2b^2 - 6b^4$ using a negative HCF.

HCF $= -2b^2$
$= -2b^2(2b - 1 + 3b^2)$
Oxford Maths 10 VC (2022), Exercise 2F Q4i (p. 78), adapted: instruction reworded for a single part
1A.4
I can substitute numbers for pronumerals and evaluate.
Short answer · 1 mk

Evaluate $yz - x$ when $x = 2$, $y = -1$ and $z = 5$.

$= (-1)(5) - 2$
$= -7$
Cambridge Essential Mathematics 9 (3rd Ed), Ch 2 review, short answer Q2a (p. 164)
Short answer · 1 mk

If $a = 1$, $b = 3$ and $c = -2$, evaluate $2a^2b + 3ac^2 - b^3$.

$= 2(1)^2(3) + 3(1)(-2)^2 - 3^3$
$= 6 + 12 - 27 = -9$
Oxford Maths 10 VC (2022), Exercise 2C Q2h (p. 59)
Short answer · 2 mk

If $a = -2$, $b = -1$ and $c = 3$, evaluate $abc - ab^2c - c^3 \div b$.

$= (-2)(-1)(3) - (-2)(-1)^2(3) - 3^3 \div (-1)$
$= 6 + 6 + 27 = 39$
Oxford Maths 10 VC (2022), Ch 2 review, short answer Q6b (p. 99)
